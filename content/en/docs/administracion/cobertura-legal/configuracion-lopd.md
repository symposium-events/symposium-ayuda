From here we control whether we want a text to be displayed in order to comply with the Spanish Data Protection Act (Ley Orgánica de Protección de Datos, LOPD), and we can also add and remove templates to be displayed.

Generally, what we will do is create templates of the LOPD text in different languages, as well as the institution's own internal privacy policy.

![](/media/admin/captura_de_pantalla_2013-03-21_a_la_s_11.41.16.png?align=center)

Once a template has been created, an event organizer can use it so that it is displayed during the sign-up or registration process for an event.
