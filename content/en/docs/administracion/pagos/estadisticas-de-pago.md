In the “Payments statistics” section you will get an overall summary of the amount collected in your events through the existing Payment methods.

![](/media/admin/captura_de_pantalla_2013-03-21_a_la_s_10.54.39.png?align=center)

In addition, you will have access to statistics showing which event has collected the most revenue and which one has received the largest number of paid registrations. ![](/media/admin/captura_de_pantalla_2013-03-21_a_la_s_10.57.55.png?align=center)
