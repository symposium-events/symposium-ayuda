Symposium supports payments through these three methods:

- **POS terminal** (virtual card payment terminal), based on:
      - Servired
      - CECA
      - ELAVON
      - OPENPAY
      - ADDON
      - BAC Credomatic
- **Bank transfer**
- **Paypal**

You can enable payment methods for your instance from Administration panel > Payments > Payment methods:

![](/media/admin/metodos_de_pago.png?w=250&align=center)

From this section you can only **enable** or **disable** the payment methods that you need your portal to support for any financial operation. Just tick the check boxes of the payment methods you need. After enabling a payment method, you must configure at least one [Payment receiver](/docs/administracion/pagos/receptores-de-pago) for it.
