This tool is used to give support to other users: you can impersonate any user registered on your institution's portal to assist them if they need help editing their events or have any other question.

Go to the menu Users > Log in as another user:

![](/media/admin/suplantar_usaurio.png?w=250&align=center)

As an Administrator, you can impersonate a user in two ways:

- With the **event ID**: this way you will open the event for editing, but with the organizer account of the user who owns it.
- With the **user's name** or **email address**: in this case you will not go directly to the event; instead you will access the private panel of the user, who may or may not be an event organizer.

![](/media/admin/suplantar_usuario.png?w=750&align=center)
