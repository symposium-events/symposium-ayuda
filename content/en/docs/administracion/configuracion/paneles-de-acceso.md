Here you can configure the login panels you need to be shown on the site's login page. In general this is limited to enabling or not the option to log in through social networks, so that external users do not necessarily have to register on the platform to access it, but can use their Google, Facebook, Twitter or other available accounts.

![](/media/admin/opciones_de_autenticacion.png?w=576&align=center)

When social login is enabled, a user with an account on the portal can have different "identities" to access the system and reach their user account. The user is always the same regardless of how many "identities" are associated with it.
