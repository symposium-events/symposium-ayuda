In the Administration panel, Tools menu, you can set up PUSH notifications to be sent to users of the Symposium mobile app:

![](/media/admin/notifiaciones_a_moviles.png?w=250&align=center)

You can send notifications to all users of the [Symposium for attendees](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=events.symposium.app) app who are registered for an event. Users of the app will receive the notification instantly through the app itself.

You can send the message in several languages. Write the subject and the message you want to send to your users and enter the translated text in as many languages as you have selected.

![](/media/panel_admin/idiomas_notificacion.png?w=691&align=center)

There may be attendees who do not speak any of the languages you have configured. In that case there is a drop-down list of options:

![](/media/panel_admin/notificacion_app_3.jpg?w=718&align=center)

Your notification will be sent immediately to all attendees of your event who have the mobile app installed.
