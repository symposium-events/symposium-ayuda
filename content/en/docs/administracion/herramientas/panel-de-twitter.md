In the Twitter section of the administration panel we can use [Twitter search operators](http://twitter.com/#!/search-home) to configure the content of the **Real time** tweets on a specific topic.

![](/media/admin/captura_de_pantalla_2013-03-21_a_la_s_09.22.23.png?align=center)

The panel is usually configured to show the tweets of the institution's Twitter account, but we could combine it with other searches or hashtags of interest.

Once configured, the Twitter panel on the portal's welcome page is updated with the tweets.

![](/media/admin/captura_de_pantalla_2013-03-21_a_la_s_09.26.45.png?align=center)
