You will find this section in the *"Tools"* menu of the Administration panel.

![](/media/notificaciones_a_moviles/menu_notificaciones_a_moviles.png?align=center)

From this section we can send notifications to all users of the mobile app for attendees. Use this feature with caution, since users may complain if we overload them with information.

We can send the notification in all the languages the platform supports. The notification must be filled in in at least one language. If we do not want to set a future sending date for the notification, we can enable the Send now option. Once the details are filled in, click Send and the notification will arrive instantly, or at the scheduled time.

![](/media/notificaciones_a_moviles/pagina_notificaciones_a_moviles.png?align=center)
