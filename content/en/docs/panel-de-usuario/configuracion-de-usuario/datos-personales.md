Access your account settings from your profile icon, at the top right of the screen > **Configuration**.

![](/media/admin/configuracion_perfil.png?w=300&align=center)

In this section you can keep your personal information up to date. This data is used to send email notifications, to identify you in an event's attendee list, or to auto-complete your details if you are a speaker at an event. You can also change your password from time to time to increase your security.

![](/media/admin/configuracion_perfil_sym.png?w=700&align=center)
