Symposium keeps you informed about everything that happens on the platform through an alert system. In the main header menu of your user panel you will find the **Notifications** panel.

![](/media/admin/notificaciones_panel.png?w=600&align=center)

![](/media/admin/notificaciones_22.png?w=700&align=center)

Here you can mark pending notifications as read and review past ones.
