The financial administrator is a role on the SYMPOSIUM platform with two main functions:

- [Assign payment receivers](/docs/gestion-economica/configuracion/asignar-receptor-de-pago)
- [Manage and check the payments received for an event.](/docs/gestion-economica/herramientas/gestionar-pagos-de-un-evento)

It can also run statistical queries.

## Accessing the financial management panel

To access the financial management panel, click the *“Financial management panel”* link in the page footer, inside the administration block. To have access to this panel, the platform administrator must first have registered the person who will act as financial administrator.

![](/media/admin/panel_economico.png?w=750&align=center)

Once inside the financial management panel, the left-hand menu shows the available options.

![](/media/admin/panel_economico_2.png?w=750&align=center)
