In the financial management panel, inside the **Information** menu, you will find this section:

![](/media/admin/resumen_del_evento.png?w=250&align=center)

This tool lets you view and export details of the registrations for the event you select. It is divided into three blocks:

- Attendees
- Registration
- Products

![](/media/panel_admin/resumen_evento.png?w=699&align=center)
