From this section you can create new expense centers and assign them to your events.

![](/media/admin/centros_de_gasto.png?w=250&align=center)

Expense centers are a Symposium feature that lets universities classify the events that receive payments according to the accounting entities in charge of managing those payments.

Here you can see all active expense centers and check the events they are associated with.

Each expense center must have an identifying name and a code.

![](/media/panel_admin/centro_gasto.png?w=504&align=center)<br>
You can add as many events as you need to the same expense center, and you can remove them at any time. Add them by entering the event title or ID.

![](/media/panel_admin/anadir_centro_gasto.png?w=729&align=center)
