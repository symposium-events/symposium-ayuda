To assign a card-payment terminal (TPV) to your event, you must first link it to an identifier. If you do not, the following message will appear in **Financial management panel** > **Configuration** > **[Assign payment receiver](/docs/gestion-economica/configuracion/asignar-receptor-de-pago):**![](/media/archivos_asistentes/identifier_1.png?w=512&align=center)You can therefore assign an identifier to your event from **Financial management panel** > **Payment code** > **Payment identifiers**:

![](/media/archivos_asistentes/identifier_2.png?w=200&align=center)First, find the event by its ID or title. Then click the **Search** button:![](/media/archivos_asistentes/identifier_3.png?w=512&align=center)Next, add the payment identifier or income code that corresponds to the event. Once saved, you can go back to **Financial management panel** > **Configuration** > **[Assign payment](/docs/gestion-economica/configuracion/asignar-receptor-de-pago)** **[receiver](/docs/gestion-economica/configuracion/asignar-receptor-de-pago)** and link your event to the TPV.

| |
|---|
| **WARNING!** **Once the event has payments, you will not be able to modify or delete its identifier.** |
