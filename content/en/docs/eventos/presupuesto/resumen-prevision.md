The **Summary and forecast** section gives you a clear, structured view of your event's financial status. Here you can record and monitor your **forecast and actual income and expenses**, making efficient budget management easier.

![](/media/archivos_asistentes/prevision_1.png?w=300&align=center)

At the top you will see the event's current balance, which is calculated automatically by subtracting actual expenses from actual income.

## Income

Lets you add all forecast income and compare it with the income actually received, recorded in the **[Income log](/docs/eventos/presupuesto/registro-ingresos)** menu.

![](/media/archivos_asistentes/ingresos_2.png?w=400&align=center)

- Category: defines the source or type of income.
- Forecast: estimated income you expect to receive.
- Actual: income received.

As shown in the image, you can add more rows or delete records with the icons available.

## Expenses

Lets you keep track of forecast expenses and those actually incurred, recorded in the **[Expense log](/docs/eventos/presupuesto/registro-gastos)** menu.

![](/media/archivos_asistentes/gastos_3.png?w=400&align=center)

- Category: type of expense.
- Forecast: budgeted expense.
- Actual: expense incurred.

In addition, for one or more registered expenses, you can create a payment order that lets you formalize expenses and ensure financial traceability. To do so, you will need to include the following information:

![](/media/archivos_asistentes/orden_4.png?w=700&align=center)

Finally, to make it easier to keep track of the event's finances, you can generate a report from the **Export** button.
