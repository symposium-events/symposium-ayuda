You can integrate your event with Twitter to broaden its reach and make it more visible to the public. Assign a hashtag to the Event in the text box of that panel and click Save.

![](/media/panel_organizador/rrss.png?w=250&align=center)

You can change it later simply by typing another one, or even remove it with the Delete button. Spread that hashtag on Twitter to spark debate and give it visibility.

![](/media/panel_organizador/twitter.png?w=750&align=center)
