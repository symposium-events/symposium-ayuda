Adding videos from YouTube is easy in Symposium. Click **Tools > Videos**. Once there, click the add video button, paste the link to the video and give it a title.

![](/media/panel_organizador/videos.png?w=750&align=center)

From here you can also edit or delete previous videos, using the corresponding buttons in the list.
