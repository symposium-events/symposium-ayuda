Check the information about the webinars held in your event and download reports with the most relevant information about the rooms and the attendees.

In the **Content > Schedule / Videoconferences** menu you will find a list with the information about the webinar rooms you have held.

![](/media/admin/informes.png?w=250&align=center)

## List of webinars held

The list shows, in order, all the webinars that have been held in the event. These reports will only be available to:

- the event owner
- members of the Administrator committee.

Users who have permission and request webinars will only be able to see the report of the webinar they requested.

### Report contents

The information shown at the first level is:

- Webinar title
- Total duration of the room in minutes
- Total number of participants (host and panelists included)

![](/media/admin/informes_webinar.png?w=891&align=center)

### Report

In addition to the information offered at the first level in the list of webinars, the report contains more data of interest. It is a downloadable Excel file organized in two tabs: Webinar summary and Attendees.

#### Webinar summary

- Event title
- Webinar title
- Total duration of the room in minutes
- Total number of attendees, counting the host and panelists

#### Attendees

- Email entered by the attendee to join the room
- Time in minutes the user stayed in the room
- Name entered by the user to join the webinar
- Number of times the attendee connected to the room
- Date and time of the connections
