## Registering for an event

The process of registering for an event is described in [this article](/docs/uso-de-la-plataforma/inscripcion-a-un-evento).

## Contacting the organizers

It is often useful for a user to be able to contact the organizer of an event. To make this possible, the tool provides visitors to the event page with a contact form that sends an email to the event organizer, either to their contact email or to the event's contact email address if one is configured.

## Editing the registration form

The answers given in a registration form can be modified. To do so, just go to our [attendee panel](/docs/panel-de-usuario/eventos-a-los-que-asisto) and from there click the **Registration** button for the corresponding event.

## Adding products to a registration

After registering for an event, we can extend the registration by adding products on sale. This process can be started in two ways: using the link in the email confirming our registration, or using the **Add products** button in our [attendee panel](/docs/panel-de-usuario/eventos-a-los-que-asisto).

## Cancelling an attendance

You can cancel your attendance at an event from our [list of attendances](/docs/panel-de-usuario/eventos-a-los-que-asisto).
