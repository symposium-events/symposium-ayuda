A traffic analyzer such as Google Analytics can be included in each project. This is a Google tool for tracking your website's traffic. It is available in the **Tools** > *Google Analytics* menu.

![](/media/crowdfunding/menu_analytics.png?w=290&align=center)

Enter in the text box your tracking ID, which the Google application will give you, and it will be included in all the pages of your project. Click **Save**.

It will give you statistics about website visitors to boost the site's visibility and appeal. Data such as their geographical origin, the words they used to find you in the search engine, or the page they came from help you improve your project's marketing.

![](/media/crowdfunding/analytics.png?w=866&align=center)
