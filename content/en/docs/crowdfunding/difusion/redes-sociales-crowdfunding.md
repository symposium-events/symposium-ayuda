You can promote your project on Twitter and make it more visible to the public. Assign it a hashtag in the text box of that panel and click **Save**.

![](/media/crowdfunding/menu_twitter.png?w=290&align=center)

You can change it later simply by typing another one, or even remove it with the **Delete** button. Spread that hashtag on Twitter to create debate and give it visibility.

![](/media/crowdfunding/difusion_twitter.png?w=866&align=center)
