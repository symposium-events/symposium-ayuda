Sometimes the author of a project needs to send a message to a participant, or send an announcement to all of them. Finding each person's address is usually a tedious, time-consuming task, but since that information is already in Symposium, you can use this tool in the **Sponsorships > Send email to donors** menu.

![](/media/crowdfunding/enviar_correo_donantes.png?w=250&align=center)

The panel is an email wizard with the usual basic controls of this kind of application:

1. **Recipient selector**
1. **Subject** of the notification
1. **Recipients who will receive a copy**
1. **Body of the message** to be sent

## Recipient selector

A list of all the project's donors is shown. You can select several of them or tick the "All donors" option to send an announcement to all of them.

## Subject

A text box for the subject of the email to be sent.

## Recipients who will receive a copy

A text box in which to type, by hand, the email addresses of the recipients you want to receive copies of the message. It is optional and is enabled by ticking a checkbox.

## Body of the message

The text is written in a box that allows several simple formatting options. As it is an email, there is no defined maximum length.
